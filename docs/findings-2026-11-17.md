# Trial moves, 2026-11-17: RunLab 0.10.0 against the real projects

The developer asked whether RunLab can be finished within the week and used by every
repository. Claude first answered from keyword counts and the repositories' own notes; asked
"are you sure about your claims?", it ran RunLab and moved three projects in scratch copies.
Five of its claims did not hold (`docs/project/failures.md` [L3]). This file holds what was
measured, the question the developer raised from it, and the route chosen. Nothing here is
built or decided unless it says who decided it.

## The question the developer raised

Asked whether a setting's help line should show on the page when Hydrofoil moves, the
developer (2026-11-17): "the fact you are asking makes me believe that we have not yet well
defined what runlab can do and is common across repos and where the line between a repo and
runlab is".

What the trials show of it: RunLab's line today is where DeePC's bench ended. What DeePC's Lab
had cut by the developer's choice for a small project (the card controls, Save, the hovers)
RunLab lacks for every project, and what DeePC's pieces never used (rows of several fields)
RunLab cannot read.

## A line, proposed by Claude, not decided

The developer on it, asked the same day through AskUserQuestion ("Is this the line?"): "Close,
discuss later". It stays a proposal, to be settled with them before anything is built.

1. **RunLab holds how a run is asked for, run, saved, listed, compared and drawn. A project
   holds what it means:** which settings, numbers and charts there are, their words, units and
   help lines, and every formula.
2. **A matter of taste is the project's switch, with RunLab's default; never RunLab's rule.**
   The page's words already work this way (R2).
3. **RunLab grows only from a real project:** (a) when one is blocked on something of RunLab's
   kind, (b) when two hold the same helper. What one project alone needs and can do itself
   stays with it, and reaches the page through what RunLab leaves open: a figure of its own,
   files of its own.

The same rules put to what the trials found:
